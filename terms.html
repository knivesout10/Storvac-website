1. Prohibited Items

To ensure the safety of all stored goods, the following items are strictly prohibited:

  • Perishables: Food or anything that can rot.
  • Hazardous Materials: Flammables, chemicals, explosives or gas cylinders (unless empty).
  • Illegal Goods: Any items prohibited by law.
  • Valuables: We strongly advise against storing high value jewelry, cash, or original legal documents (passports, certificates).

2. Packing Requirements

  • All items must be securely packed in suitcases, double walled boxes, or check bags.
  • StorVac Logistics reserves the right to refuse items that are poorly packed or leaking.

3. Payment & Fees

  • A full payment is required to secure a slot.

Late Fees

  • Items not collected within 7 days of the agreed return date will incur a weekly storage fee of 50% of the total amount payable.

Refunds

4. Pickup & Delivery of Logistics

  • Customers or a designated representative must be present at the agreed location/pickup point during the scheduled pickup.
  • Items will be delivered at the same point of pickup upon reopening at no fee.
  • If a customer does not show up for either pickup or delivery, a rescheduling fee of 50% of total amount payable will apply for the next attempt.
